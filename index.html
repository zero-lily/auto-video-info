About Auto_Video

Auto_Video is an internally developed video production and publishing application. It is used only by its owner to assist with video-processing workflows such as preparing edited video files, subtitles and metadata, and publishing completed videos to YouTube channels managed by the owner and authorized through Google OAuth 2.0.

The application is operated locally and exclusively by its owner. It is not provided to external users. It may be used with multiple YouTube channels managed by the owner, provided the required Google OAuth 2.0 authorization has been granted. Auto_Video does not request or store Google or YouTube account passwords.

YouTube API Services

Auto_Video uses YouTube API Services for authorized YouTube operations, including publishing and managing video-related information when those functions are used.

Use of Auto_Video is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service. For information about Google's handling of data, see the Google Privacy Policy.

Access and availability

The executable application is not publicly hosted, distributed, or made available to third parties. This website exists only to describe the application, its use of YouTube API Services, and its privacy and usage terms for compliance purposes.
